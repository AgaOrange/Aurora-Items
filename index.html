Rarity letters: C common, U uncommon, R rare, VR very rare, L legendary, A artifact, ± varies by version.

Points: common 0, uncommon 1, rare 3, very rare 5, legendary 7, out of a budget of your level + 3 (prestige levels included). "Slot" means it's a consumable and counts toward the 2 × proficiency consumable cap instead. Rank 1 games allow up to uncommon; Rank 2 games up to rare.

Banned items from the Aurora ban list are hidden. Tick "show banned items" to see them crossed out. No item can let you cast a spell of 7th level or higher, even if it normally would.

Adventure items come only from adventure books, so they also need to be on the Allowed Adventure Items List. Griffon's Saddlebag Book Two is items only. Only 2014 books are listed, since 2024 content isn't allowed. D&D Beyond also files the DMG's basic items under the Basic Rules; those show as DMG here.

Reading the text: "free to read" items open in full on D&D Beyond with no purchase. Book Two items link to the publisher's free posts. Everything else opens in full on D&D Beyond if your campaign shares the book.

Source: names, rarity, type, attunement and tags from D&D Beyond's public item lists, pulled 7 October 2026.
